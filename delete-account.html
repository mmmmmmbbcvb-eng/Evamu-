Delete your account

How to delete your Evamu account and data

1
In the app
Open Evamu, go to Profile, tap the menu, choose Settings, then Delete account and follow the confirmation.
2
Can't open the app?
E-mail YOUR_EMAIL@example.com from the address you signed up with, or tell us your Evamu username. Write "Delete my account" in the subject.
3
What we delete
Your profile, reviews, lists, follows, favorites, listening history, streak, taste profile and settings. Your sign-in account is removed from Firebase Authentication.
4
What may remain
Replies and likes other people made on your content may remain without your name.
5
How long
We handle e-mail requests as soon as we can and confirm by e-mail.
