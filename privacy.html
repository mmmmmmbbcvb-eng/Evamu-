Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains what Evamu stores about you and how it is used. Last updated: October 2026.

What we store

Account: your e-mail and sign-in method, kept by Firebase Authentication. Profile: display name, username, bio, website and social links, profile photo and banner. Activity: your ratings, reviews, replies, likes, lists, follows, favorites, want-to-listen songs, listening history and streak.

Your e-mail

Your e-mail stays in Firebase Authentication. It is not saved in the public profile that other signed-in users can read.

Recommendations

Evamu keeps a taste profile (songs, artists and searches you interact with) to suggest music. Only you can read it, and you can erase it in Settings > Feed and content > Reset recommendations.

What other people can see

Signed-in users can see your profile, reviews, ratings, lists and who you follow. Your blocked and muted lists, bookmarks and notifications are visible only to you.

Services we use

Firebase (Google) for sign-in and data storage. Public music catalogs (Apple iTunes Search and Deezer) for song and artist information and preview clips. Those requests contain your search terms and the songs you open, not your account details.

Reports and feedback

When you send a report or feedback, we store its text, your account ID and the time, so we can review it.

Your controls

Edit your profile in Settings > Edit profile. Clear local copies in Settings > Data and storage. Block or mute people. Delete your reviews and lists yourself at any time.

Local data

Evamu keeps cached artwork, offline copies and your settings on your phone. Logging out clears the cached copies.

Deleting your data

You can delete your reviews and lists inside the app at any time. To ask for your whole account and its data to be deleted, follow the steps on the account deletion page (delete-account.html) or e-mail us. We will confirm by e-mail when it is done.

Children

Evamu is not meant for young children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.
