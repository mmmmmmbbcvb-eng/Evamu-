The complete Evamu guide

The complete Evamu guide. This page explains the app's screens and features and how to use them. Button names may vary slightly by version. Some actions require an internet connection and a signed-in account.

1. Getting Started and Home

Home combines music discovery and social activity. For You shows suggested content based on your activity and musical interests, while Following shows reviews from accounts you follow. Pull down to refresh. Animated placeholders mean content is loading; if loading fails, check your connection and try again.

2. Search

Use Search to find songs, artists, people, and reviews. Switch between the available result tabs rather than relying on only one type. Opening a song takes you to its details; opening a person takes you to their profile; opening a review takes you to its discussion thread. Song details, artwork, and previews may come from external music catalogs, so availability can vary by song or region.

3. Song Details

The song page displays song information, artwork, available ratings, and reviews. You can rate a song from half a star to five stars and then write and publish your opinion. Check your rating before submitting. If you have already rated the song, look for your rating summary and use the edit or delete controls when available. A saved review may take a short time to appear.

4. Writing a Helpful Review

Focus on the song itself: its melody, performance, lyrics, production, or the feeling it gives you. Be clear and respectful, and avoid posting other people's private information. Your numeric rating expresses your own opinion; it is not an official ranking. You can reopen a review later to follow replies and interactions.

5. Review Thread and Replies

Open a review or tap its comment icon to enter the dedicated discussion page. Read replies, type into the reply field, and send your response. If you are replying to a specific person, check the reply-to label before sending. Like, reply, and view counts may appear depending on available data. If a reply does not appear, refresh the page and check your connection.

6. Likes and Bookmarks

The heart button adds or removes your like. The bookmark button saves an item for later or removes it from your saved items. A like is an interaction; a bookmark helps you return to content later. Counts may update after data synchronizes with the server.

7. Profile

Your profile can show your name, username, photo, bio, links, stats, reviews, pinned songs, and features such as Streak or your music taste summary when available. Tap Edit profile to update your information. You may be able to open the banner image for a larger view. Do not put private information in your public bio.

8. Following People

Open someone's profile and tap Follow. Tap the button again to unfollow if that option is shown. Following lists the accounts you follow; Followers lists the people who follow you. After following someone, their reviews may appear in your Following feed. If they do not appear immediately, refresh the feed and verify that you followed the correct account.

9. Music Lists

A list is a collection of songs organized under a name, description, and optional artwork. Open Lists to browse available lists, open a list, or create one. The detail page shows its owner, description, songs, and possibly likes, bookmarks, and collaborators. Public lists may be visible to other users according to the app's settings. Private lists are intended for restricted access; do not assume a private list is public just because someone shared a link.

10. Creating a List

Open Lists and choose Create list. Enter a clear name, add an optional description, choose Public or Private, and add songs using Add Music. Review the song order and list details, then tap Save. If saving fails, check that the list has a name and that you are online. You can later edit the list if you own it and editing controls are available.

11. Inviting a Friend to a Shared List

Open a list you own, then open its options or collaborator/member management. Choose the person you want to invite from the available accounts and tap Invite. The invitation may show as Invited while you wait for their response. Your friend should open the list or invitation and choose Accept to collaborate or Decline to refuse. After accepting, they should appear as a collaborator and can add songs according to the list's permissions. If you cannot find the person, verify their username and make sure their account exists. Never share your password; invitations should be handled inside the app.

12. Shared List Permissions

The owner created the list and can manage or delete it. An accepted collaborator can add songs according to the list's rules. A viewer who is neither owner nor collaborator cannot add songs. Deleting a list removes the list and its ordering, but does not delete the original songs or their reviews from Evamu. Confirm that you are deleting the correct list before proceeding.

13. Notifications

Notifications may show likes, replies, mentions, new followers, and list activity when those events are available. Tap a notification to open its related item when a destination is available. In Settings > Notifications, you can enable or disable notification categories. Android has separate system notification settings, so phone notifications may remain disabled even when a category is enabled in Evamu.

14. Account Settings

Use Settings > Edit profile to change your name, bio, photo, and links. Account information shows the account details available in the app. Change password is intended for accounts that use email and password; Google accounts manage their passwords through Google. Never send your password to anyone or share password-reset links.

15. Blocking and Muting

Blocking hides a blocked account from you in the parts of the app that apply the block filter. Muting reduces the appearance of a person's reviews or notifications; it is not the same as unfollowing them. Open Blocked accounts or Muted accounts in Settings to manage these lists. If older content remains visible, refresh the page; previously loaded items may remain until reloaded.

16. Reporting a Problem or Content

Use Settings > Report a problem to describe an issue clearly. To report a person or list, use the report option in its menu when available. Explain what happened and the steps that led to it. Do not include your password or verification codes. Reporting does not automatically block someone; use Block as well if you want to hide that account from yourself.

17. Streak — How It Increases

Streak tracks consecutive days of music-rating activity. Rate a song each day to maintain your streak. The flame's appearance or color may change as you reach higher levels. Best is your highest recorded streak, while Current is your active streak, so Best may be higher than Current. If the counter does not update immediately, allow time for synchronization or reopen your profile.

18. Streak Grace Period and Streak Freeze

In this version's Streak logic, there is a grace period after your last activity, and Freeze items can extend that period. Freeze items may be earned after reaching certain streak milestones, with up to two stored. A Freeze does not add free days to your streak count; it extends the time before the streak expires. Watch the flame status and remaining time if shown. When the grace period ends, the current streak may stop or reset according to the system state.

19. Flame Styles and Levels

Some flame appearances unlock as your streak progresses. You may be able to use the default appearance, select an unlocked style, or choose a style that matches your musical taste. A locked style is not necessarily an error; you may need a higher best streak to unlock it. You cannot select a style that has not been unlocked.

20. Weekly Challenges

Your profile or challenge card may show weekly goals, such as rating a number of songs, discovering artists or genres, or writing reviews. Open the challenge card to see the target and your progress. Challenges reset according to their weekly cycle, and activity may count only after it has been saved successfully. If you complete a goal but progress does not change, reopen the card and check your connection.

21. Music Taste and Recommendations

Evamu may use interactions such as searches, song opens, listening, ratings, reviews, and favorites to understand your taste and suggest relevant music. Recommendations are suggestions, not a final judgment of your taste. To start fresh, open Settings > Feed and content > Reset recommendations if the option is available. Resetting recommendations does not necessarily delete your reviews or lists.

22. Player and Music Previews

The app may show a mini-player or song preview when a supported catalog link is available. A play button does not mean Evamu owns or provides the full music file; some songs have no preview. If playback stops, check your connection and volume and make sure the app is not restricted in the background. Closing a page may not stop playback if a separate music service is running.

23. Year in Review

If Year in Review is available, it summarizes your music activity for a selected year, such as reviews, artists, or songs you interacted with, based on recorded data. Results may be limited for new accounts or when there is not enough activity. You can share the summary card using the available sharing options. Review the card before sharing because it may show your username or musical activity.

24. Favorites and Want to Listen

Adding a song to Favorites or Want to Listen records a preference that helps you return to it and may improve recommendations. This is different from rating a song or writing a review. Use the same feature button to add or remove the item. You may need to reopen the song to synchronize its status.

25. Cache and Weak Connections

The app may keep artwork and some display data locally to speed up loading and reduce network requests. Old data may temporarily appear when you lose connection. Settings > Data and storage > Clear cache removes temporary files; it is not intended to delete your reviews or account. Images may load more slowly the next time after the cache is cleared.

26. Reduce Motion

Enable Reduce motion under Settings > Appearance to reduce some transition animations. This option is for visual comfort and should not change account data or ratings. If a particular effect does not change, that effect may not be controlled by this setting.

27. Sign-in and Common Login Problems

Check your email and password, or choose the sign-in method you used when creating the account. If email verification is required, open the verification message, follow its link, and return to the app. If sign-in fails, check your connection, make sure the app is up to date, and remove any extra spaces from the email address. Do not create a second account before confirming your original sign-in method.

28. Missing Data

If a review, list, or follow does not appear, pull to refresh, check your connection, confirm that you are signed into the correct account, and reopen the page. Privacy settings, blocking, or an unaccepted collaboration invitation may prevent some actions. If an error appears, send it through Report a problem with the page name and the steps that caused it.

29. Privacy and What Others Can See

Treat your public profile, reviews, and public lists as content other users may see. Do not post sensitive information in your name, bio, or reviews. A Private list setting is intended to restrict access according to the list system, but it is not a reason to share sensitive content or links carelessly. Read the Privacy Policy for a description of the data the app uses.

30. Log Out and Delete Account

Logging out ends the account session on this device; it does not necessarily delete the account or all server data. In this version, Delete account may show an explanatory message instead of performing a complete deletion. Read the confirmation shown and do not assume your account has been deleted unless the app explicitly confirms it. To request permanent data deletion, use the available support channel and ask for clear confirmation.

31. Sending Useful Feedback

Include the page name, what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and the steps needed to reproduce the issue. Include the error text if one appeared. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or payment information. Use Settings > Send feedback or Report a problem depending on the type of message.

32. Important Reminder

Evamu is a social music app. Some features depend on sign-in, Firebase rules, connectivity, and external catalog data. If a button or option is missing in your version, the feature may not be available in that context or may require owner/collaborator permissions. Do not assume an action succeeded until a confirmation appears or you can see the saved change.
